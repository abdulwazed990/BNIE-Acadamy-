Bangladesh National Institute of Education (BNIE) — বাংলাদেশ জাতীয় শিক্ষা ইনস্টিটিউট

Official Portal (https://bnie-my-gov-portal.com/) — Government-Approved Autonomous National Educational Institute in Bangladesh

Bangladesh National Institute of Education (BNIE Bangladesh / বাংলাদেশ ন্যাশনাল ইনস্টিটিউট অফ এডুকেশন) is an autonomous educational institute established and officially approved by the Government of the People's Republic of Bangladesh on 15 July 1996 (১৫ জুলাই ১৯৯৬) under Government Institutional License No: BNIE/GOV-LIC/1996/0715, Official Registration No: REG-BNIE-1996-0715, and Government Order / Memo Ref: MOE/REG-1996/0715-BNIE.

Core Academic & Student Verification Services

  • Certificate Verification Online — SSC, HSC Vocational & Diploma Certificate Verification (অনলাইন সনদ যাচাই)
  • Online Marksheet Verification — Honours, Degree Pass & Masters Result Check (অনলাইন মার্কশিট যাচাই)
  • Diploma in Engineering & Polytechnic Diploma Courses (Computer, Civil, Electrical, Mechanical)
  • Academic Programs Catalog — Bachelor Honours, Degree Pass, Masters & Vocational Education
  • Online Admission Application Bangladesh — Diploma & Honours Admission (অনলাইন ভর্তি আবেদন)
  • SSC Vocational & HSC Vocational Specializations (কারিগরি ও ভোকেশনাল শিক্ষা)
  • BNIE Student Services & Digital Academic Transcripts
  • Government Approval & Institutional License Registry (Approved 15 July 1996 — BNIE/GOV-LIC/1996/0715)
  • BNIE Help Center, Student Verification Guide & FAQs
